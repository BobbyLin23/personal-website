Hi, I'm Bobby Lin. I build beautiful, performant web applications and love sharing what I learn along the way.

## What I do

My focus is full-stack web development with a modern stack — Nuxt and Vue on the front end, serverless backends, and AI-assisted workflows. I care deeply about developer experience, performance, and clean interfaces.

## What I care about

I'm passionate about open source, writing, and AI. I believe tools should make people more capable, not replace them. Most of my work is about building products that feel simple on the surface while solving real problems underneath.

## What I write about

On this site I share weekly reports on my learning and progress, plus longer posts about web development, design, and technology. If something interesting crosses my desk, I try to write it down and pass it along.

## Get in touch

The best way to reach me is on [GitHub](https://github.com/bobbylin23) or [X](https://x.com/BobbyLin23). I'm always happy to talk about open source, web development, or AI.
